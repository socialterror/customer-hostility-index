In addition, as a result of the sharp decline in commodity prices in early March 2020, which decline continued for most of the second quarter of 2020, we recorded a non-cash ceiling test impairment for the six months ended June 30, 2020 of approximately $3.5 billion, of which approximately $2.5 billion was recorded for the three months ended June 30, 2020 and approximately $1.0 billion was recorded for the three months ended March 31, 2020.
