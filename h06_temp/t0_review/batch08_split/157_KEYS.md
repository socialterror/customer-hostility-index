## F5
F5 SP002 [5] (renewal,renewals) | We only defer these costs when we have determined the commissions are, in fact, incremental and would not have been incurred absent the customer contract. Capitalized incremental costs are allocated to the individual performance obligations in proportion to the transaction price allocated to each performance obligation and amortized based on the pattern of performance for the underlying performance obligation. Contract costs related to initial contracts and renewals are amortized over the same period because the commissions paid on both the initial contract and renewals are commensurate with one another.
