We believe that higher sales growth rates for certain product offerings, such as equipment, building materials and above-ground pools and spas evidence increased spending in traditionally discretionary areas, such as pool construction, pool remodeling and equipment upgrades. In the second quarter of 2020, sales for equipment, which includes swimming pool heaters, pumps, lights and filters, increased 22% compared to the same period last year.
