Commodity Prices We are exposed to market risk related to price fluctuations in scrap metal and other metals.
- However, there is typically a lag between the effect on our revenue from metal price fluctuations and inventory cost changes, and there is no guarantee that the vehicle costs will decrease or increase at the same rate as the metals prices.
